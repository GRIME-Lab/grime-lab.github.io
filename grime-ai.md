GRIME AI is open-source software for working with environmental time-lapse imagery, from download to AI segmentation.

![GRIME AI USGS Sites tab showing the camera site list, site details, and a midday image from the Mendenhall River](/assets/images/grime-ai-usgs-sites-tab.png)

*The USGS Sites tab: browse more than 1,100 USGS cameras, view each site's details and available gage data, and download images for a date range.*

## Features

- **Retrieves imagery** from USGS HIVIS, PhenoCam, NEON, and state mesonets
- **Quality-controls images** automatically
- **Explores image features** such as greenness (GCC), texture, and color clusters
- **Trains and runs AI segmentation models** (SAM2, SAM3, SegFormer)
